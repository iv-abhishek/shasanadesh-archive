The printed rule (FHB p.183) gives "three months" and a limit of three times in service;
the current position (Vitta Path 2011 p.9–10) is 180 days for confinement, no three-times
limit, not admissible with two or more living children, two years' gap between two
maternity leaves, and six weeks for miscarriage/abortion as printed. Lead with the current
position.
