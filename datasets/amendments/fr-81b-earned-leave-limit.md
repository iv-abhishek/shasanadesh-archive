FHB p.71 prints 180 days as the limit at which a government servant ceases to earn earned
leave; the 1992 amendment (FHB p.302–303) raised it to 240 days from 01.01.1987; the current
limit is 300 days (Vitta Path 2011 p.4). Leave encashment up to 300 days is a separate
provision (Vitta Path p.2).
