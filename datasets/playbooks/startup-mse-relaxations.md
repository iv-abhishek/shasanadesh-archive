## How to answer
- Split the answer by relaxation: (1) EMD / bid security exemption, (2) prior experience, (3) prior turnover, (4) performance security. Say for each whether it applies to MSEs, startups or both, and the conditions (Udyam registration and manufacturer of the offered item; DPIIT recognition certificate).
- UP departments: UP GeM GO dated 26.11.2024 paras 43 and 47 — note that the experience/turnover relaxation in para 47 is limited to MSEs and startups located in Uttar Pradesh, the manpower-outsourcing limit it names, and the buyer's option to prefer experienced sellers for safety/security-critical purchases with approval.
- Central: GFR 2017 Rule 170 (EMD exemption) and Rule 173(i) (startup relaxation); the manuals say the relaxation must not compromise quality or technical specifications.

## Traps
- Performance security is not waived for these categories in the UP GeM GO; say so if asked.
- State buyers may limit the GeM MSE EMD exemption to MSEs of their own State through ATC (GeM GTC note).
