## How to answer
- Give the turnover condition as a share of the estimated cost over the stated number of years, with the page's own worked example when there is one (UP GeM GO dated 26.11.2024 para 45 for UP GeM bids; Works manual for works, including the bid-capacity formula; Non-Consultancy manual for services, including its upper cap).
- Say who may relax it and how (the approval level named in the UP GO), and that startups/MSEs may be relaxed.
- If asked "as per GFR", say the percentages come from the procurement manuals issued under GFR 2017, not from a numbered GFR rule.
