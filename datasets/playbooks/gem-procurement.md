## How to answer
- Give the value bands and the mode for each: direct purchase from any seller meeting quality and delivery, L1 purchase among a minimum number of sellers/OEMs, and above the top band mandatory bidding or reverse auction (GFR 2017 Rule 149; UP GeM GO dated 26.11.2024 para 6).
- UP: Bid to RA is mandatory above the stated value (UP GO para 7), with the exception for unavoidable cases added by the UP GO dated 11.03.2025.
- Mention that demand must not be split to avoid the bid/RA route (GFR 149(viii)), and reasonableness of price must be checked.

## Traps
- This playbook is for general GeM questions. Narrow GeM questions (EMD, ePBG, turnover, experience, single bid) have their own playbooks.
