## How to answer
- Match the source to the question. "As per GeM GTC" or any GeM bid → answer from GeM GTC 4.0 para (m) first (pages 18–19). "As per GFR" → GFR 2017 Rule 170. UP department buying on GeM → also the UP GeM GO dated 26.11.2024, para 43. Open tender outside GeM in UP → the UP Procurement Manual (Goods) 2016, para 9.7 and nearby.
- Cover, in this order: whether EMD is taken at all (GeM: no bid security below the threshold on page 18), the amount (GeM recommended rate and allowed range; GFR range), validity, who is exempt (list the exempt categories exactly as the page lists them), when it is forfeited, and when it is refunded.
- Exemption questions ("are PSUs / MSEs / startups exempt") get a direct yes/no first, then the exact category line and its conditions (e.g. Udyam registration and manufacturer of the offered item for MSEs; DPIIT certificate for startups). Mention the GTC note that exempt sellers cannot be asked for EMD through ATC, and the State-buyer option about MSE EMD.
- In limited tender enquiry, bid security is normally not taken (goods manual 4.8, works manual LTE section).

## Traps
- Do not answer a GeM question only from the UP Procurement Manual 2016; GeM bids follow GeM GTC, and the UP GeM GO says it prevails over the 2016 manual for GeM purchases.
- Bid security (EMD) and performance security (ePBG) are different; do not mix their percentages.
