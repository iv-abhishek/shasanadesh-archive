## How to answer
- Start with the usual rule: completed similar works/services in the stated recent period — one of a high share, two of a middle share, or three of a lower share of the estimated cost — quoting the exact percentages and years from the page you cite (Works manual for works, Non-Consultancy manual for services, Goods manual annexure for goods).
- UP departments buying on GeM: UP GeM GO dated 26.11.2024 para 46 (services: the one/two/three-work rule with its worked example; goods: past supply as a share of tendered quantity). Changes need the approval level named there.
- If asked "as per GFR": say plainly that GFR 2017 sets the principles (Rule 173 — fair, non-restrictive criteria; relaxation for startups), and the percentages come from the Ministry of Finance procurement manuals issued under GFR. Do not invent a GFR rule number for the percentages.
- End with the startup/MSE relaxation in one line and point to the turnover criteria as the companion condition.

## Traps
- Do not mix the works percentages with goods; goods use quantity-based experience.
- "Similar work" must be defined in the bid document; mention it.
