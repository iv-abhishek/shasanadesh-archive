## How to answer
- GFR 2017 Rule 172(1): payments normally only after supply/service; the kinds of cases where advance may be needed; the ceilings for private firms, for Government agencies/PSUs, and for maintenance contracts; who may relax the ceilings; and the bank guarantee safeguard. Rule 172(2): part payment after despatch, as per contract terms.
- Add the Goods manual section 6.5 conditions if the question asks about procedure.
