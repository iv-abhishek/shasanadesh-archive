## How to answer
- GFR 2017 Rule 162: the value limit (note the amended figure and the higher limit for scientific departments in the footnote), sending to registered suppliers, the minimum number of firms, publication on GeM/CPPP and own website, no unsolicited bids, and the circumstances allowing LTE above the limit.
- Manuals: bid security and performance security are normally not taken in LTE; risks such as splitting demand.
