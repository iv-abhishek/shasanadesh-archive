## How to answer
- GFR 2017 Rule 173(xx): lack of competition is not decided only by the number of bids; a single bid may be valid if the three conditions are met (adequate advertisement and time, criteria not unduly restrictive, reasonable price).
- UP: UP GeM GO dated 11.03.2025 (replacing para 16 of the 26.11.2024 GO) — the bid evaluation committee checks the three conditions and sends the case with justification to the authority one level higher, who decides to accept or re-invite; if the conditions are not met, re-invite.
- Goods manual: a single offer is treated like a single tender for delegation of powers; routine re-tendering is discouraged.
