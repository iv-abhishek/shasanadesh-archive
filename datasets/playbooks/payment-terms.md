## How to answer
- GeM purchase → GeM GTC 4.0 clause 12 "Payment Authority and Payment Terms" (page 30): when
  payment is due for goods and for services, and what it is counted from (consignee receipt-
  cum-acceptance certificate). UP departments on GeM → also UP GeM GO dated 26.11.2024 (the
  time limit for paying service providers on page 13).
- GFR 2017 Rule 172: payment normally only after supply/service; advance and part payment as
  exceptions (refer to the advance-payment playbook for the ceilings).
- Manual for Procurement of Goods 2024, chapter 6.3–6.4: payment terms must be stated in the
  contract and kept; modes of payment and documents for domestic and imported goods.
- Answer in that order of authority; say which rule is GeM-specific.
