## How to answer
- GFR 2017 Rule 166: the three situations allowing single-source procurement (sole manufacturer, emergency with reasons recorded, standardisation on technical advice), and the PAC that must be given for 166(i) and (iii) — list its contents.
- Goods manual 4.10 (PAC procurement) and 4.11 (single tender without PAC) for procedure; Annexure 9 for the PAC format.

## Traps
- A single tender (only one firm invited) is different from receiving a single bid in an open tender; point to the single-bid rule if the question is really about that.
