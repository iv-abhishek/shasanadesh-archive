## How to answer
- Answer from the Manual for Procurement of Works 2025, section 5.3.1: when it may be given (specialised, capital-intensive works, provided in the contract), the rate of the contract price, that it is interest-bearing, the bank guarantee required, release in instalments against utilisation certificates, and recovery.
- Point out the difference with GFR 2017 Rule 172 (advance payment to suppliers of goods/services) only if the question mixes them.
- If the question names a UP department or a specific UP order, say these pages give the central rule, and that the department's own order (if any) should be checked.
